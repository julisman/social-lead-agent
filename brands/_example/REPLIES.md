# {{DISPLAY_NAME}} — reply templates

**This file is deliberately not generated from config, and should not become a script.**

These are *shapes*, not text to paste. Every reply must name something specific from the
person's post — the area, the dates, the budget, the detail they mentioned. A reply that
could be pasted under any post reads as a bot, and people say so in public, under your
reply, where everyone can see it.

Log which template you used in `actions.jsonl`. Never the same template twice in a row.

**No links. No phone numbers. Bio only.** (DMs are different — see `social-dm`.)

### `rec-timing` — they mentioned dates, arrival, or urgency

Acknowledge the timing first — it is the thing they actually care about.

> {acknowledge the date}. {what you can do by then}. {soft invite}

---

### `answer-first` — they asked a general question

Answer it honestly, **including when the honest answer is not "buy from us"**. This
template converts worst and builds the account best. It is also the only one that works
on crowded posts, where every other reply is a pitch.

> {genuine answer, 1-2 sentences, with specifics}. {one soft line, or none at all}

---

### `en-visitor` — they wrote in English

> {answer in English}. {soft invite}

---

### `light` — saturated post, or a low-effort question

One short line, no pitch. Sometimes being useful and leaving is the right move — they
can find the bio.

> {one useful line}

---

## Openings to rotate

List five or six. Vary the **first three words** — the opening is what makes repetition
visible to a human scrolling a feed, far more than the body does.

`opening A` · `opening B` · `{direct answer, no greeting}` · `opening D`

## Never

- Opening with the business name
- Corporate register ("We provide…", "Our services include…")
- More than one emoji
- Repeating a template with only the place name swapped — the single most detectable
  pattern, and the one that gets an account reported by a person rather than flagged by
  an algorithm
